How much should the community levy be?

Estimate what a proposed UK data centre will use and emit, put a price on it with the government's own figures, and set a community levy against that cost. The UK Data Centre Covenant asks strategic sites for a Local Benefit Plan "linked to the development's size" but sets no formula. This gives you one, and shows exactly how every number is worked out.
